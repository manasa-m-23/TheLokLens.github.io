About

Making Public Policy Understandable Through Research, Data & Analytics

LokLens is an independent initiative dedicated to transforming complex public policy issues into accessible, evidence-based insights through research, data visualization, and policy analytics.

“A society where people clearly understand the civic issues that shape their lives and are empowered to engage with them thoughtfully.”

“To create accessible, citizen-focused learning spaces that explore public issues through data, dialogue, discussions and shared insight that enable people to better understand challenges and contribute to informed civic awareness.”
